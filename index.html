Our story

Started in a home kitchen, built by two bakers.

Maison Miel began as weekend bread sold out of a Hauz Khas apartment. Three years later, we still bake in small batches — no mass ovens, no shortcuts, just dough given the time it needs.

Every loaf is shaped by hand before sunrise, and every table is set for people who want to actually sit down.
